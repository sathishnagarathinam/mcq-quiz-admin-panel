Account & Data Deletion Request

Request deletion of your account and associated data

Important: This page allows you to request the deletion of your Dakshin Postal Academy app account and all associated personal data. Please read the information below carefully before proceeding.

🗑️ How to Request Account Deletion

Step 1: Send an email to our data protection team at sssmcqpost@gmail.com
Step 2: Include your registered phone number or email address in the request
Step 3: Specify "Account Deletion Request" in the subject line
Step 4: We will process your request within 30 days and send you a confirmation

📧 Contact Information

Email: sssmcqpost@gmail.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request

Response Time: Within 30 days

Support: sssmcqpost@gmail.com

📧 Send Deletion Request Email

📊 Data That Will Be Deleted

Data Type Description Deletion Status
Personal Information Name, phone number, email address ✅ Permanently Deleted
Quiz Performance Scores, progress, quiz history ✅ Permanently Deleted
App Usage Data Login history, app interactions ✅ Permanently Deleted
Profile Data Profile pictures, preferences ✅ Permanently Deleted
Payment History Transaction records, purchase history ⚠️ Retained for 7 years (Legal Requirement)
Support Communications Customer service emails, feedback ⚠️ Retained for 3 years (Business Requirement)

⏰ Data Retention Periods

Immediate Deletion (0-30 days): Personal information, quiz data, app usage data, profile data
3 Years Retention: Customer support communications (for service quality and dispute resolution)
7 Years Retention: Payment and transaction records (required by Indian financial regulations)
⚠️ Important Notice: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. You will lose access to all your quiz progress, scores, and purchased content. If you wish to use the app again, you will need to create a new account.

🔒 What Happens After Deletion

  • Your account will be immediately deactivated
  • You will no longer be able to log into the app
  • All personal data will be permanently deleted within 30 days
  • You will receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete
  • Some data may be retained as required by law (see table above)

📱 Alternative Options

Before requesting deletion, consider these alternatives:

Account Deactivation: Temporarily disable your account without deleting data
Data Export: Download your quiz progress and scores before deletion
Privacy Settings: Adjust your privacy settings in the app
