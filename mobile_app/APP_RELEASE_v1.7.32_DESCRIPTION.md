# 📱 Dakshin Postal Academy - Version 1.7.32+40

## 🎯 App Overview

**Dakshin Postal Academy** is a comprehensive mobile application designed for complete preparation of Post Office departmental exams. The app provides extensive MCQ practice tests, real-time performance analytics, and interactive learning features.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📚 **Comprehensive Quiz System**
- **Free & Paid Quizzes**: Access free practice tests or purchase premium exams
- **30-Day Access**: Paid quizzes remain accessible for 30 days after purchase
- **Admin-Granted Free Access**: Admins can grant free access to specific users for paid quizzes
- **Real-time Quiz Notifications**: Receive instant notifications when new quizzes are available

### 💳 **Secure Payment Integration**
- **Multiple Payment Options**: Razorpay, PhonePe, and UPI support
- **Secure Transactions**: All payments are encrypted and verified via webhooks
- **Payment History**: Track all your purchases and payment status

### 📊 **Performance Analytics**
- **Detailed Score Reports**: View comprehensive analysis of your quiz performance
- **Attempt History**: Track all quiz attempts with timestamps
- **Progress Tracking**: Monitor your improvement over time
- **Category-wise Performance**: Analyze performance by exam category

### 🔐 **Security & Privacy**
- **Screenshot Prevention**: Prevents unauthorized screenshots and screen recording
- **Secure Authentication**: Email-based login with OTP verification
- **Data Protection**: All personal data is encrypted and securely stored
- **Privacy Compliant**: Fully compliant with Google Play Store privacy policies

### 🎓 **Exam Hub**
- **News & Updates**: Latest exam news and announcements
- **Tips & Shortcuts**: Expert tips for exam preparation
- **Previous Year Papers**: Access to past exam papers
- **Results**: View exam results and performance metrics

### 🔔 **Smart Notifications**
- **Quiz Alerts**: Get notified when new quizzes are available
- **Payment Reminders**: Reminders for quiz access expiry
- **Direct Navigation**: Tap notifications to go directly to quiz pages

## 🚀 Installation & Setup

### From Google Play Store
1. Open Google Play Store
2. Search for "Dakshin Postal Academy"
3. Tap "Install"
4. Wait for installation to complete
5. Tap "Open" to launch the app

### Manual Installation (APK/AAB)
1. Download the app bundle or APK file
2. Enable "Unknown Sources" in Settings → Security
3. Open the downloaded file
4. Tap "Install"
5. Launch the app once installation is complete
